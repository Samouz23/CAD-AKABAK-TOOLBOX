Page-Frequencies

Home Desktop

List of frequencies as specified in dialog Frequencies.

Selecting will change the frequency of the Field-display if such a Field has been specified at Page-Observations. If you select multiple entries AKABAK calculates the rms-mean-value. For extended selection use the mouse together with then SHIFT- or CTRL-key, respectively. Alternatively, use UP- and DOWN-key and menu Processing/Field show. Selecting a header results in the mean-value of all results with frequencies in the associated bin.

Flat displays a flat list of frequency values. Decades creates bins of decades, for example, 10, 100, 1k, etc. Octave creates bins of octaves, for example, 250, 500, 1k, 2k, etc. Octave 1/3 creates bins of 3rd of an octave, for example, 100, 125, 160, 320, etc.
