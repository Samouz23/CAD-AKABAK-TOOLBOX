Form - Driven Boundaries

Home Sub-Form

In this form you specify details of driving-motion of boundary elements. The motion of the boundary is typically associated with the movement of a diaphragm. The diaphragm can be a loudspeaker membrane, but it can also be a virtual plane, the driving surface of a horn-throat, for example.

All boundary elements can be assigned a driving. You assign the driving on page Boundaries by selecting Type of Boundary = Driven.

The driving-motion is assumed to be constant across a plane.

By default the motion is assumed to be normal to the plane. However you may want to specify a different direction of motion by opening this Driven Boundaries Form by clicking the button Options if Boundary is Driven - Edit.

Direction of Driving

Normal to Surface means that the motion is in parallel to the normal of the boundary element. This is the default setting.

Specified Direction means that the vibration is projected into a certain direction. Way of Specification gives a range of options to specify this direction, see Frame - Direction.

Modification of Driving Values

At solving stage the driving velocity is v = 1 m/s. This is the default value. At observation stage this value will be multiplied by the corresponding parameter of the lumped element network or of the fixed driving table.

Amplitude

Value of amplification. By default the amplitude is v = 1 m/s.

Delay

Value of signal delay τ in seconds:

v2 = v1·exp(-j·ω·τ)
