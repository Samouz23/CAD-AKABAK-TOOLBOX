Acoustic Parameters of Air

Home Sub-Form

Tool to calculate the speed of sound c and the density ρ of air. Called from Form - Acoustic Parameters.


Formula for static pressure as given in Wikipedia:

P = P0·(1 - 0.0065·H)/(T0 + T)5.255

with pressure at sea-level P0 = 101325Pa, temperature T in degree Celsius and T0 = 273.15C

The speed of sound is approximately

c = c0·√(T/T0 + 1)

with speed of sound c0 at T = 0C.

The density of air is approximately

ρ = ρ0·P/P0·T0/(T0 + T)
