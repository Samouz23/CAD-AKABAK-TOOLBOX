Form - Fields (BE Ref)

Home Observation

Fields (BE Ref) calculates the field at the boundaries using the BE-mesh.

Create a new Field-component with the help of the New Component Form. The home of the component is page Observation. Edit an existing component by dbl-click its entry on page Observation or issue menu Edit/Parameters or press CTRL+E.

The result of the Field calculation is the frequency response of the sound pressure. It will be displayed as a contour-plot in the 3D-viewer.

The frequency range is specified at menu Global/Frequencies. After calculation select the frequency or frequency-range to plot at Desktop-Page - Frequencies.

The check-boxes in the list at the side of the desktop lets you show or hide the field. Un-checked Field-records will be skipped at next round of calculation.

Note, for viewing you may get a better picture if you hide the original boundary in the viewer by un-checking its entry in the component table at page BEM. Alternatively, check Views/Boundaries/Wire Frame.
