Formula can be used for inputting floating point numbers for making the project parameterized.

At certain components Formula can be used to specify the transfer-function of a component. In this case we have a Runtime-Formula.

Formula at component-level can access the formula-system specified globally. Open the global formula-editor at menu Global/Formula.


Component-Forms of the General and BEM sections provide a special page labeled Formula. Formula entered here will forward results to the associate input-fields.

Components of the LEM-part provide an entry labeled Formula, which opens a formula-editor. Results will be assigned to parameters of the component.


Formula have a specific syntax and they provide a set of in-build functions. Reserved identifiers link the result of the formula to a parameter of the component.
