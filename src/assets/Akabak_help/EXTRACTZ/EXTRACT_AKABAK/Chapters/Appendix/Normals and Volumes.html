Interface Elements

The normal is an orientation-vector, which points perpendicular to the surface of a boundary element. For each BEM-boundary you need to provide the principal orientation of this normal.

The principal orientation of the normal can have two spins because there are two sides to a boundary element. As explained further down the spin of the normal is derived from the order of specification of the vertices.

For the Boundary Element Method the normals are playing an important role, because it is through the normals the solver knows about the enclosed volume. In general, the BEM produces a field only for the volume, which is completely surrounded by a boundary. In case of radiation into open space, one of the boundaries is at infinity assuming a Sommerfeld radiation-condition. Outside the boundary the result of the Helmholtz-Integral yields zero pressure. For Direct-Sound-Analysis normals are of no importance.

In AKABAK normals should point always into the domain, which the element belongs to. For an exterior domain this volume is the outer space extending to infinity. An exterior domain is specified by Element Type = Exterior of section Subdomain. In AKABAK a Domain is an individual radiation volume. A Subdomain is similar, however a Subdomain is coupled to other Subdomains.
