In the axis-symmetric case there are only two indices of points, P1 and P2, with P = (Axial, Radial)T. These points mark the edges of concentric rings. These rings generate the surface-area of truncated cone sections.

The normal of a cone-section points inwards, i.e. towards the Axial-axis, if Δa > 0

The order of the specification of nodes controls the principal direction of the normal, which should point always into the domain. The domain is the volume enclosed by the cone-sections. For interior problems the normals should point into the volume enclosed, with exterior radiation conditions the normals point away from the body.
