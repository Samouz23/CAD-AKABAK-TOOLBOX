Extrusion means that a shape is expanded into another dimension. For example a line becomes a plane, a plane becomes a body or a contour becomes a rotational body. The application of extrusion is typically used for Dimension=3D.

Rotational or Circular

Assuming you want to draw a boundary of circular shape. Of course you can do this with the help of Nodes and Elements or with the help of an external CAD-application by using mesh-files. However, the Extrusion feature may help to facilitate drawing a rotational plane. Here the idea is that we draw a simple outline or contour. Firstly, we specify a chain of Nodes with the help of the Nodes-Form. In a second step these coordinates are put together to line segments with the help of the Elements (Nodes) - Form.
The contour can be anywhere in space, however, often it is more convenient to draw it on the principal axis. If necessary the coordinates can eventually be shifted and rotated with the help of Scaling.
Important to note is that the first and last line-segments have a special meaning. These line-segments always have one end on the axis of rotation. The first line starts with P0 which is on the axis. The last line ends with Pn which is also on the axis of rotation. By this the axis of rotation gets specified, and secondly, we can select whether the final shape is open or features closing caps.

For example, to the right we see Nodes prepared to create a horn of circular cross-section. The first node with name P1001 is at the origin. The last node with name P1006 is on the x-axis. Hence, in this example the axis of rotation is the x-axis. In the Elements (Nodes) - Form we specify a list of line-segments which eventually becomes the contour of our rotational form. There are always only two node-numbers. The order matters. The final step is to specify the kind of rotation and its parameters. The dedicated Extrusion-Form can be called via the sandwich-menu or by dbl-clicking on the label "Extrusion...". Note, that Extrusions have their own Swap-Normals control.

Linear

Line to Plane

In the Elements (Nodes) - Form we specify two nodes for a line-segment. Together with the Height parameter of the Extrusion-Form a plane is created. The size of the extrusion is controlled by the Height-parameter, the orientation by parameter Direction.

Plane to Body

If the shape of specified at the Elements (Nodes) - Form is a plane then the extrusion produces a body. For example one can enter the floor of a room and then use Extrusion to raise the walls and ceiling of specified Height. Direction=Normal means that the extrusion goes into the direction of the plane.
