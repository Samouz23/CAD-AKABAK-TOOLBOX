A Script is a means to specify components and its parameters. In AKABAK the script itself is regarded a component. As such the script forms a group for its components. For example, a script-component which is assigned to a subdomain or an interface makes that all its components also belong to the subdomain or interface, respectively.

There are two types of script-syntax: one is used in the BEM-part and the other at the LEM-part.
