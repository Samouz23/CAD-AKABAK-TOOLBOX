The direction of the normal is given by the order of the vertices of a boundary element. It is specified by the Right Hand Rule as sketched in the figures. If you form with your right hand a fist with thumb up then this indicates the order in which vertices should be specified. The starting vertex is arbitrary.

Normal of a Triangle

If P1, P2 and P3 are the three vectors of a triangle then the normal is

N = (P2 - P1) x (P3 - P1)

eventually N needs to be normalized.
