Editing of a List

Home

The GUI-system of AKABAK makes use of list-controls.

Navigation

Moving

Navigation inside the table means to move the input-focus, which is indicated by a blue background and white font.

You can click inside the field rectangle or use the left, right, up and down cursor-keys.

Multi-Selection

Multi-Selection means that multiple rows can be selected for further processing, for example for copy- and paste operations. Range-Selection: Select the first row, press and hold the SHIFT-key and then click on the last row to be selected. Pick-Selection: Press and hold the CTRL-key. Then click on the items you want to select respective un-select.

Editing

String or Number Fields

Parameters such as the Amplification, Delay or the Comment of the Fixed Driving Form can be entered with the help of an inline-editor.

There are several ways to invoke the editor. First move the focus to the field of the list with a mouse-click or by using the cursor-keys. Second click again or press the key F2. You may also use following short-cut: You can directly start typing. The edit field with focus will go into edit mode and receives your input.

Submit entered values by clicking somewhere else, pressing ENTER or by exiting the field with the help of a cursor-key.

To abandon edits press the ESC-key.

Lookup Lists

If a selection is to be made the edit-field comes in the form of an expanding lookup-list.

You open the list in the same way as the number-editor. Click twice or press F2 for the focused field. Eventually the lookup-list opens from where you can select.

Submit entered values by clicking somewhere else or by pressing ENTER.

To abandon edits press the ESC-key.
