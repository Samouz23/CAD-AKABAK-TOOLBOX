Purpose A simplified meshing is applied
Used in Diaphragm, Shell

Simplified Model

In this case a simpler shape is produced by the Diaphragm- or Shell-factory, which often is sufficient, especially at low frequencies.

In this mode only Meshing=Bifu is possible. More on meshing see at Form-Meshing Also only circular shapes will be meshed.

The specification of the width of the suspension is ignored and assumed to be part of the outer dimension of the diaphragm. It is common practice to add half of the suspension-width on both sides, or in other words dD = dD0 + ws

Also magnet details are not available in this mode.

Note, the Simplified Model makes a correction internally. This correction has to do with the coupling between the BEM- and LEM-parts. The idea is to produce an approximation to a circular diaphragm which yields quick calculation for the BE-part. However, in order to get correct mechano-acoustic driving the coupling factor is deduced not from the mesh but from the specified diameter.

Standard Model

The standard way of modeling creates a mesh taking into account all specified details of Diaphragm or Shell. This includes the width of the suspension, poles, exclusions as well as the magnet (if Side=Rear).
