Form - Nodes-Group

Home General Sub-Form of Nodes

Specify size and position of a fictional shape, which vertices become nodes in the associated Nodes-Form.

Note, that Group is only creating points. No shape is created, yet. The actual shape is created by the component, which makes use of the Nodes-object.

Group Index

Main-index of group of coordinates, which can be used by other components as reference. The Group-Index should be unique.

Comment

A string for an optional annotation.

Group

Shape of the component

Direction

The principal orientation of the shape is specified by the direction of its normal. More sophisticated directions are available at Scaling (see below).

Origin

The anchor for the position of the shape. The position-coordinates can be entered at Scaling (see below).

Width, Height

The size of the shape.

Scaling

Specify position-coordinates and rotation-angles with the help of the Scaling-Form.
