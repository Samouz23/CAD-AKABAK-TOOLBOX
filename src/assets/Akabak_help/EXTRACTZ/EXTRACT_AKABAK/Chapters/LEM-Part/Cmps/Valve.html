Valve

Home LE-Part Electric

The Valve element implements a small signal, broad band model of a vacuum tube similar to the FET. Pole s is the Grid, pole t the Anode and pole u the Cathode of the Valve.

Parameters

Caption String Name of component
Formula String Formula of parameters
g S Trans-conductance
Rac Ω Anode-Cathode resistance
ft Hz Transition frequency
Cag F Anode-grid capacitance

The resistance at the input terminal of a Valve tends to infinity and is not specified.

The frequency-dependency of the Valve is simulated with the help of the transition frequency ft, the trans-conductance g and the Anode-Grid capacitance Cag.

Y1 = jω·Ccg with Ccg = g/(2π·ft) (Cathode-Grid capacitance)

Y2 = jω·Cag with Cag Anode-Grid capacitance

Y3 = 1/Rac with Rac Anode-Cathode resistance
