The lumped element Diaphragm is a transducer. It couples the mechanical and acoustic domains. We assume a mobility network type for the mechanical and an impedance network for the acoustical domain.

The mechanic terminals are to the left. The flow is the force f and the potential is the velocity v.

To the right are the acoustic terminals. The top one stands for the front side of the diaphragm, whereas the bottom terminal stands for the rear side of the diaphragm. In-between is the acoustic ground. The acoustic flow-parameter is qf which is the volume-velocity at the front side. Accordingly is qr the flow parameter at the rear-side. The potentials pf and pr are sound pressure parameters, at the front and at the rear side, respectively.
