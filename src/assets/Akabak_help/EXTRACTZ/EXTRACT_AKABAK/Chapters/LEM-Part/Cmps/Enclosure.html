Enclosure Sealed

Home LE-Part Acoustic

The Enclosure Sealed-component can be based on lumped elements or use a more complex rectangular enclosure with standing waves. In the world of lumped elements the Enclosure is specified as a two-pole, where one pole is always grounded.

Parameters

Caption String Name of component
Formula String Formula of parameters
Vb m3 Volume
Lb m Depth of enclosure
eta Damping factor η

Vb

Vb is the volume of the enclosure which yields an acoustic Compliance at low frequencies.

Lb

Lb specifies the depth of the enclosure assuming a rectangular shape.
If Lb is specified the internal model is of a closed duct of length equal to Lb and of cross-section Vb/Lb.
If Lb = 0 then a lumped element model is used for the enclosure. The associated lumped element network features the acoustic compliance Ca and resistance Ra.

Ca = Vb/(ρ·c2) Ra = ρ·c/(η·Vb)

with air-density ρ, speed of sound c

eta

Eta specifies enclosure losses in the same way as explained at component Duct.
