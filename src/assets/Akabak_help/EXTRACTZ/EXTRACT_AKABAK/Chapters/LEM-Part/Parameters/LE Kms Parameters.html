This form lets you input Kms as explained in LE Mech Parameters.

Additional there is in-build a little calculator for common equivalent parameters. These can be entered in this form. However they would be converted to Kms after applying the OK-button.

Kms

Kms is the effective stiffness of all suspensions and possible other springiness. The unit of Kms is N/m.

Cms

Cms is the mechanic compliance: Kms = 1/Cms. The unit of Cms is m/N.

Vas

Vas is called the equivalent volume of unit m3. It is an informal parameter. Here the idea is to express the stiffness of the transducer-suspension as a volume of air, which would have the same stiffness if compressed. In this way it is convenient to select the acoustic enclosure for this speaker. This is so because

fc = γ · fs Qtc = γ · Qts γ = √(1 + Vas/Vb)

with fc system resonance frequency of speaker in closed enclosure, Qtc total quality factor and Vb volume of enclosure [33].

Vas is deduced from the equivalent acoustic compliance of a closed enclosure of volume Vas:

Cas = Vas/(ρ0·c02) Kms = 1/(Cas · SD2)

with ρ0 density of air and c0 speed of sound. Of course, for conversion, values for ρ0 and c0 should be those at the time of measurement of Vas. This calculator uses fixed values for the conversion of Vas to Kms of ρ0 = 1.2 kg/m3 and c0 = 343 m/s.
SD is the effective radiating area of the membrane, which is typically the area projected in axial direction. From the suspension only half its width is taken into account.
