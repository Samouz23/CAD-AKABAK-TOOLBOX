Form - Infinite Baffle

Home BEM-Part
       
   

Component Infinite Baffle (IB) creates a special Radiation Condition, where we install a reflecting wall of infinite extend inside the exterior domain. In AKABAK you can specify even two Infinite Baffles, one for the front and one for the rear, and each can be displaced.

The IB is home to a particular Subdomain which should have Type of Domain=Exterior.

In general the IB is a practical device often applied in analytical acoustics and for measurements. This is so because, first, the special boundary condition of zero normal-velocity at the IB can give simple analytical solutions. Second, the IB avoids diffraction effects of an enclosure, and hides away the rear of the radiator, which often is not of interest. See also chapter Infinite Baffle in General.

Create a new Infinite Baffle-component with the help of the New Component Form. The home of a Infinite Baffle-component is page BEM. Edit an existing component by dbl-click its entry on page BEM or issue menu Edit/Parameters or press CTRL+E.

Page - Name

On page Name provide the name of the component in form of a string. Further there is space for annotations.

Page - Position

An IB has a direction, which is specified with the help of Form - Direction.

The normal of the Infinite Baffle matters, it should point into the domain, i.e. into the halve space where the radiation is, see chapter Normals and Volumes.

The 3D-viewer of AKABAK displays the IB with the help of a large lucent plane. If you enable Views/Structure/Normals then you can see the normals of the IB at the corners.
Note, because the IB-plane is transparent it can happen that its visual presence mingles with the colors of the observation Field. A little rotation of the object will reveal the arte-facts.
