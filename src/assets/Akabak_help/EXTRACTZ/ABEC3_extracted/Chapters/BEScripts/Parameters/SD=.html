Identifier SD=
Units m2
Format Floating point number
Purpose Projected area of circular shape without the suspension.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell
See also Diaphragm shapes, dD=, ws=

SD= is the total area of a circular diaphragm, see sketches in Diaphragm shapes.

For exclusion of an inner area use SD1= or dD1= with a negative value.

Normally, the width of the suspension is taken into account with the help of parameter ws=. Only for the Simple= mode you may include the width of the suspension in SD=.
