Identifier dD1=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Diameter of dust-cap.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell
See also Diaphragm shapes, SD1=, hD1=

dD1= is the diameter of the dustcap of a cone-shaped Diaphragm or Shell.

A negative value of dD1= means exclusion of a circular area in the center of the Diaphragm or Shell.
