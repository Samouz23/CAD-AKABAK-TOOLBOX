List of Radiation_Impedance Items

Home » Scripts » Radiation_Impedance

Purpose List of radiation impedance observation points.
Used in Radiation_Impedance

The list of observation points follows the parameter part of the Radiation_Impedance-section. Each line represents the frequency response of the radiation impedance of certain Driving Groups.

Each item of the line should be separated by a blank or tab.

    <Index>    <DrvGroup1>   <DrvGroup2>      ID=

Index

The first entry is an index, which becomes the "name" of the spectrum.

DrvGroup1 and DrvGroup2

The second and third parameter are Driving Groups. For self-radiation-impedance both parameters are identical. For mutual-radiation-impedance they are different, indicating power transfer between DrvGroup1 and DrvGroup2.

ID=

ID= is an arbitrary and unique string which links an ABEC-data-set to a VACS curve.


Example


  Radiation_Impedance
    GraphHeader="Radiation Impedance"
    BodeType=Complex
    RadImpType=Normalized
    101   1001  1001   ID=101
    102   1001  1002   ID=102
    103   1002  1002   ID=103

Curve #101 is the self-radiation impedance of a driving group with index DrvGroup=1001. This can be the front side of a Diaphragm, for example.

Curve #103 is the self-radiation-impedance of DrvGroup=1002, say, the rear of the Diaphragm.

Curve #102 is then the mutual-radiation-impedance from the front to the rear side. The mutual radiation impedance is a measure of the energy exchange between two radiators.
