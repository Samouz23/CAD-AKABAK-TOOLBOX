IBPlane=

Home » Scripts

Identifier IBPlane= (depreciated)
Format Identical to Position=
Purpose Enabling and orientation of Infinite Baffle
Used in Subdomain_Properties
See also Infinite_Baffle

IBPlane= is a parameter of Subdomain_Properties. If specified an Infinite Baffle will be installed for its sub-domain.

IBPlane= is regarded depreciated. In future use section Infinite_Baffle instead.
