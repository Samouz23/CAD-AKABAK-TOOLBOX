Identifier hD2=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Height of the cone on the rear-side of a Diaphragm.
Used in Diaphragm
See also Diaphragm shapes, tD1=

hD2= measures the outside of the cone of a Diaphragm from the outer suspension downto the voice-coil mounting point.

hD2= has only an effect for Diaphragm with parameter Side=Rear.
