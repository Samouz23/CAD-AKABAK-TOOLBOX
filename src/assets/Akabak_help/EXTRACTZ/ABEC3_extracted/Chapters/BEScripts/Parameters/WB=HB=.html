Identifier WB=; HB=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Width and Height of Baffle.
Used in Baffle
See also RectType=, dB=

WB= and HB= are the outer dimensions of a rectangular shaped Baffle. WB= is the width and HB= is the height.

The particular shape can be selected with the help of parameter RectType=.

Provide also Position= to specify mounting point and orientation of the Baffle.
