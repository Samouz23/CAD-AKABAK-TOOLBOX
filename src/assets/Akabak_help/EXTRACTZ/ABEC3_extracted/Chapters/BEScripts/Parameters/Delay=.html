Delay=

Home » Scripts

Identifier Delay=
Units s
Format Floating point number
Purpose Individual delay of item-values
Used in List of Driving Elements, List of Pressure Points, List of Driving Values
See also Weight=

Delay= allows to add time-delay to the driving-value. Delay= is used in item-lists of Driving_Values, Driving and Pressure_Points. For details see chapter Delay Example.

The total time delay is the sum of the value given in the parameter- part and at list-items:

  Delaytot = DrvDelay + Delay

Example

  Driving_Values
    DrvType=Pressure
    DrvDelay=1ms
    Value=1.0
    101   DrvGroup=1001   Weight=1.0   Delay=1ms
    102   DrvGroup=1002   Weight=1.0   Delay=2ms

The total delay for item 101 is 2ms because Delay= is declared in the parameter-part and in the list-item. The total delay for item 102 is 3ms.
