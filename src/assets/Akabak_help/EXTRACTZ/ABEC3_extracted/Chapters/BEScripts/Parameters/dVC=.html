Identifier dVC=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Diameter of voice-coil.
Used in Diaphragm
See also Diaphragm shapes, hVC=

dVC= measures the diameter of the voice-coil cylinder on the outer side of a cone-shaped Diaphragm.

Side=Front

You need to specify dVC= also for Side=Front if HasPole=true. In this case the pole automatically is assigned the diameter of dVC=.

Side=Rear

dVC= is the diameter of the voice-coil former at the junction voice-coil - cone. In this way dVC= specifies the base diameter of the total cone and the diameter of the voice coil stub (if present, see hVC=).

If there is no magnet (dM=0 or hM=0) then the hole is closed by a non-vibrating boundary.

If there is no magnet and dVC < 0 then the hole is left open.

There is no dedicated pole for the Side=Rear, however a pole can be approximated by using the magnet parameters or hVC=.
