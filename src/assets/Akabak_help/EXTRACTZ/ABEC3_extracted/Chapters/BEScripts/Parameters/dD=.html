Identifier dD=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Diameter of outer shape without the suspension.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell, Pressure_Box
See also Diaphragm shapes, SD=, WD= HD=, ws=

dD= is the outer diameter of a circular diaphragm, see sketches in Diaphragm shapes.

Normally, the width of the suspension is taken into account with the help of parameter ws=. Only for the Simple= mode you may include the width of the suspension in dD=.
