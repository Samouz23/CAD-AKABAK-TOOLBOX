Identifier hVC=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Height of visible part of voice-coil.
Used in Diaphragm
See also Diaphragm shapes, dVC=

hVC= is the height of the "visible" part of the voice-coil former on the outside. By default hVC=0.

The motion of this particular part of the diaphragm is always tangential. Because of this the voice-coil former appears as a non-vibrating boundary.
