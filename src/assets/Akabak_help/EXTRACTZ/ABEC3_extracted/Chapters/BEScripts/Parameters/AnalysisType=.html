AnalysisType= is used at observation-stage, which controls the parameter you want to calculate.

There are two sets, one for the observation of the acoustic-field of Boundary Element or Direct Sound calculations and one for inspecting the Lumped Element Network.

Field

Field calculates field-values for the display in the 3D-Viewer.

AnalysisType=Pressure is the default value.

AnalysisType = Pressure

With AnalysisType=Pressure the sound pressure at field-points is calculated. Use the parameter BodeType= in order to select the way complex values are rendered, for example BodeType=LeveldB displays the standard sound pressure level Lp (SPL).

AnalysisType = DrvVelocity

AnalysisType=DrvVelocity displays the normalized velocity distribution of section VibFile. In section Field you need to add a line with RefElements=.

BE_Spectrum

BE_Spectrum calculates spectra of field-values as curve. Currently only AnalysisType=Pressure is available.

AnalysisType=Pressure is the default value.

AnalysisType = Pressure

With AnalysisType=Pressure the sound pressure response at a certain coordinate is calculated.

LE_Spectrum

LE_Spectrum calculates spectra of inspecting points of the Lumped Element Network. AnalysisType= controls the parameter of this inspecting point.

AnalysisType = Power

AnalysisType=Power calculates the power response in or at a component:

P = 1/2·Flow'·Potential

The factor "1/2" is used here because ABEC/VACS displays peak-values for physical values (voltage, force, pressure). Flow and potential are current and voltage in the electrical domain, velocity and force in the mechanical and volume-velocity and sound-pressure in the acoustical domain.

The component is specified by System=, ElName= and Port= as explained in List of LE_Spectrum Items

If no ElName= is given then the total input power of the System= is calculated.

Port=0 calculates the electrical power in the voice-coils resistance(s) Re=.

The unit is W.

AnalysisType = [Impedance, Admittance]

AnalysisType=Impedance or AnalysisType=Admittance calculates the driving point impedance or admittance of the network given by System=. Example see here.

Units are ohm or S, respectively.

AnalysisType = [Voltage, Force, Pressure]

AnalysisType=Voltage, AnalysisType=Force or AnalysisType=Pressure calculates the potential at a network node to ground or a potential-difference between two nodes.

The nodes are identified by

  • System= and Node=
  • System=, ElName= and Port=

as explained in List of LE_Spectrum Items

If no ElName= and Node= is given then the potential at the System= input is calculated.

Units are V, N or Pa, respectively.

AnalysisType = Current

AnalysisType=Current calculates the electrical current into a component.

The exact terminal is identified by System=, ElName= and Port=.

If no ElName= is given then the total input current to the System= is calculated.

The unit is A.

AnalysisType = [Excursion, Velocity, Acceleration]

AnalysisType=Excursion, AnalysisType=Velocity or AnalysisType=Acceleration calculates the motion as a flow into a mechanical component.

The exact terminal is identified by System=, ElName= and Port=.

If no ElName= is given then the total input motion to the System= is calculated.

Port=0 calculates the motion of the diaphragm of components Driver, Speaker and Diaphragm.

Units are m, m/s or m/s2, respectively.

AnalysisType = VolVelocity

AnalysisType=VolVelocity calculates the volume-velocity into an acoustical component.

The exact terminal is identified by System=, ElName= and Port=.

If no ElName= is given then the total input volume-velocity to the System= is calculated.

The units is m3/s.
