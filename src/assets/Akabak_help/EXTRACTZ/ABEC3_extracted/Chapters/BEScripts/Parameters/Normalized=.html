Identifier Normalized=
Format [true, false]
Purpose Specification if given wall-impedance is normalized
Used in WallImpedance
See also ImpType=

The WallImpedance-section defines elements with a surface impedance:

Zs  =  Z0·Zn

with Zs = p/vn is the specific impedance, p is the sound pressure and vn is the normal (particle) velocity at the surface of the element. Z0 = ρc is the plane-wave impedance and Zn is the normalized specific impedance.

Normalized=true specifies that given impedance values are regarded as being normalized, i.e. they are equal to Zn. Normalized values will be expanded internally. Normalized= is used for single values as well as for linked data-files.

Normalized= has only an effect on ImpType=Impedance or ImpType=Admittance.
