Identifier LineDir=
Format [Horizontal, Vertical]
Purpose Direction of line-source
Used in Pressure_Box
See also LineLength=, LineNumPoints=

LineDir= specifies the direction of the point source generating line of Pressure_Box.

With LineDir=Horizontal all points are distributed along a horizontal line with respect to Pressure_Box.

LineDir=Vertical distributes in the other direction.

See also chapter LineLength= for further details.
