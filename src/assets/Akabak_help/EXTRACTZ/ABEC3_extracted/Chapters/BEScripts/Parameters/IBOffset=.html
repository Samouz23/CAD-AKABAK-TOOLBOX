Identifier IBOffset= (depreciated)
Units m
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Offset of Infinite Baffle
Used in Subdomain_Properties
See also Infinite_Baffle

IBOffset= offsets the Infinite Baffle along its normal direction

For example

  Subdomain_Properties
    Subdomain=1
    ElType=Exterior
    IBPlane=z
    IBOffset=10cm

IBOffset= is regarded depreciated. Instead used section Infinite_Baffle. For example

  Subdomain_Properties
    Subdomain=1
    ElType=Exterior
    ...
  Infinite_Baffle
    Subdomain=1
    Position=z offset=10cm
