MeshFrequency=

Identifier MeshFrequency= SampleFrequ= (depreciated)
Units Hz
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Frequency criterion for meshing
Used in Control_Solver, Subdomain_Properties, Elements, Baffle, Diaphragm, Shell, Field
See also EdgeLength=

MeshFrequency= is a means to control the density of the mesh as discussed in chapter Meshing. MeshFrequency= expects a frequency value, for example

  Control_Solver
    MeshFrequency=3kHz
    Meshing=Delaunay
    ...

Typically MeshFrequency= is specified at section Control_Solver. In this way it forms a default value. It can be over-written by any further specification of MeshFrequency= or EdgeLength=. For example

  Elements
    RefNodes="N"
    SubDomain=1
    EdgeLength=4cm
    103   2001  4001  4002  2002
    ...

MeshFrequency= is an alternative way of specifying the parameter EdgeLength=, which specifies the maximum side-length of a mesh-triangle. The relation between MeshFrequency= and EdgeLength= is

EdgeLength  =  1/2 · c/MeshFrequency

Experience shows that a good starting point is to set MeshFrequency= to the upper frequency of solving, see parameters f1= and f2=. Depending on the character of the boundaries, simulation may require a higher value of MeshFrequency= or it turns out that a lower is sufficient.

Note, MeshFrequency= and the above formula is for convenience, to provide a more related parameter for controlling mesh-density than EdgeLength=. It is not deduced from strict physical limitations. The formula is a heuristic rule and can be used as a rule of thumb.


Note, MeshFrequency= can trap you in a pitfall if we consider low frequencies. Specifying a low value for MeshFrequency= can yield a situation where the structure is meshed too coarse. For example, for a sub-woofer model the high frequency response may not be of interest. One then is inveigled to lower the value of MeshFrequency= to such an extend that the generated mesh becomes too coarse for the integral method. Another, more extreme, example is the case of analyzing at DC (zero frequency), which is possible. It is immediately clear that here the meaning of MeshFrequency= is out of place.
