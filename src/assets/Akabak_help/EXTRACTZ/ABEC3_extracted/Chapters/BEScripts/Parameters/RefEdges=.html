RefEdges=

Home » Scripts

Identifier RefEdges=
Format Two integer numbers
Purpose Reference to an edge
Used in Transition

RefEdges= refers to a particular edge of a component. The component Transition uses the points on this edge as start and end points.

The edge-index has different meaning, depending on the type of component:


RefEdge= 1 2 3
Duct Edge in plane of mounting Far away edge
Baffle Outer edge Outer edge at tB<>0
Shell Outer edge Edge of baffle Inner edge
Diaphragm Outer edge Edge of baffle Inner edge


Example 1

This example demonstrates the modeling of a compression chamber. Transition links the outer edge of an elliptical Diaphragm to a Duct of elliptical cross-section.


  Diaphragm  "Diaph"
    Side=Front
    DrvGroup=1001
    WD=750mm;     HD=450mm;   RectType=Ellipse
    dD1=-120mm;   hD1=250mm;  tD1=30mm
    tB=20mm;
    HasPole=true;   HasBaffle=false;

  Duct  "Duct"
    DuctType=Ellipse
    WD=250mm; HD=100mm;   Len=100mm
    t1=250mm

  Transition
    RefElements=Diaph,Duct
    RefEdges=2,2
    SwapNormals

The Transition-elements are created from rays starting at "Diaph" and ending at "Duct". In both cases edge #2 is addressed. For the Diaphragm edge #2 means the edge, which goes around the baffle. Because HasBaffle=false there is no baffle attached to the Diaphragm. However, with tB=20mm there is a recess specified. In this case edge #2 goes around the end of the recess. For the Duct edge #2 is the edge, which goes around the second aperture, opposite the mounting point.

Example 2

In this example the Transition is used for coupling two Duct-components.


  Duct  "Duct_1"
    DuctType=Rectangle
    WD=150mm;  HD=50mm;  Len=200mm

  Duct  "Duct_2"
    DuctType=Oval
    WD=370mm;  HD=120mm;  Len=200mm
    t1=450mm

  Transition
    RefElements=Duct_2,Duct_1
    RefEdges=2,1

The transition is from Duct_2 to Duct_1. Of Duct_2 the second edge is used and for Duct_1 the first.
