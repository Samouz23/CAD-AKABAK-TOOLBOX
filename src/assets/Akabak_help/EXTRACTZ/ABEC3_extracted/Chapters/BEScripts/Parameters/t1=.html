Identifier t1=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Axial offset.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell, VibFile
See also Position=

t1= is an optional displacement in axial direction for sections Diaphragm, Shell and VibFile.

The displacement includes the baffle if specified, see HasBaffle=



Tipp: In modeling loudspeaker cabinets often we have the case that the diaphragm radiates with its front side into the exterior and with its rear into the cabinet. Hence we have to deal with the thickness of the wall of the cabinet, which typically is way larger than the thickness of the diaphragm.

Of course, one way would be to model a little tunnel, with the help of, for example, tB= (see remarks there). However often we do not need so much of an accuracy. In this case we can simply displace with t1= the rear diaphragm "artificially" so it fits the wall thickness of the enclosure.

For example

  Diaphragm  "Front"
    DrvGroup=1001
    SubDomain=1
    EdgeLength=20mm
    Position=1001,1002,1003
    NoBaffle
    Side=Front
    dD=120mm;  tD1=20mm;  dD1=20mm;  hD1=5mm
    Ws=5mm
    hD2=40mm;  dVC=20mm; hVC=0mm
    dM=80mm;   hM=20mm


  Diaphragm "Rear"
    RefDiaphragm="Front"
    SubDomain=2
    DrvGroup=1002
    Side=Rear
    t1=-10mm
