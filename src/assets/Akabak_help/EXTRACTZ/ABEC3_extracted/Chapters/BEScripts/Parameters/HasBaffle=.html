Identifier HasBaffle=
Format Boolean
Default HasBaffle=true
Purpose If true then shape is surrounded by a rectangular baffle
Used in Diaphragm, Shell, VibFile
See also NoBaffle=, tB=

HasBaffle=true or simply HasBaflle creates a rectangular baffle around the circular, elliptical or oval Diaphragm, Shell or around the shape of a VibFile, which size is identical to the outer-diameters of the Diaphragm, Shell of VibFile. HasBaffle= helps modeling the transition between circular shape and an otherwise rectangular environment.

Alternatively, you can specify also

NoBaffle=

Example with a Baffle

  VibFile "Vib"
    SubDomain=1
    VibFileAlias=Vib
    DrvGroup=1001
    HasBaffle
    NormType=PosPolar 12.8mm 0deg

Example without a Baffle

  VibFile "Vib"
    SubDomain=1
    VibFileAlias=Vib
    DrvGroup=1001
    NoBaffle
    NormType=PosPolar 12.8mm 0deg
