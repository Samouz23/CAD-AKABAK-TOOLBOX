Identifier SD1=
Units m2
Format Floating point number
Purpose Projected area of dust-cap.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell
See also Diaphragm shapes, dD1=, hD1=

SD1= is the equivalent area of a circular dome or exclusion of diameter dD1=.

As with dD1=, a negative value of SD1= means exclusion of a circular area in the center of the Diaphragm or Shell.
