Identifier RadImpType=
Format [Normalized, Specific, Acoustical, Mechanical]
Purpose Normalization of radiation impedance
Used in Radiation_Impedance

RadImpType= belongs to section Radiation_Impedance. This parameter controls the way the impedance is normalized in the output-curve.

RadImpType=Normalized

This is the default value. As explained in appendix Radiation Impedance the asymptotic value at high frequencies is identical for free radiating devices. With RadImpType=Normalized this asymptotic value becomes one. The relation between the normalized specific and the acoustic impedance is:

z = za·S/(ρ·c)

with effective radiating area S, density of air ρ and speed of sound c.

RadImpType=Specific

With RadImpType=Specific the output of the radiation impedance is according to

zs = p/v = za·S

with sound pressure p and particle velocity v. The unit is [zs] = Pa·s/m

The asymptote of specific radiation impedance is ρ·c at high frequencies and for free radiation.

RadImpType=Acoustical

With RadImpType=Acoustical the output of the radiation impedance is according to

za = p/V

with sound pressure p and volume-velocity V. The unit is [za] = Pa·s/m3

RadImpType=Mechanical

With RadImpType=Mechanical the output of the radiation impedance is according to

zm = F/v = za·S2

with force F and velocity v. The unit is [zm] = N·s/m
