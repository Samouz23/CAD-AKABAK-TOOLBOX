Identifier BodeType=
Format [LeveldB, Ampl, Real, Imag, Phase,
LeveldB_Phase, Ampl_Phase, Complex]
Purpose View of the complex-valued result
Used in Field, BE_Spectrum, Radiation_Impedance, LE_Spectrum

BodeType= means here the way complex-valued numbers are displayed in Field- and Spectrum-Observations.

ABEC calculates with complex numbers, which consists of two parts:

z = a + j·b

with the real-part a and the imaginary part b and the imaginary unit j = √-1.

A complex number can be transformed to polar coordinates:

z = A·exp(j·φ)

with amplitude A2 = a2 + b2 and phase φ = arctan(b/a)

BodeType=LeveldB

LeveldB helps to compress a wide range of values because of its logarithmic transformation. The dB scale is typically used to display the amplitude of sound pressure fields and transfer-functions in general.

The dB scale results from a logarithmic transformation of power

Lw = 10·log(P/P0)

with P0 some reference power.

If we assume the same working resistance for P and P0 then we get the dB scale for potentials and flows

LdB = 20·log(U/U0)

with potential or flow U and reference value U0.

The sound pressure level Lp or SPL is defined as

Lp = 20·log(p/p0)

with sound pressure p and reference pressure p0. It is defined as the sound pressure at threshold of hearing. For rms-values of p this reference is p0 = 20·μPa. For sinoidal signals and for peak values of p this reference is p0 = √2·20·μPa

BodeType=Ampl

BodeType=Ampl displays the amplitude-response of sound pressure fields and spectral curves. The unit is according to the observation point. For example, Pascal for sound pressure and m/s for velocity and so on. In ABEC the amplitude is a peak-value.

BodeType=Phase

BodeType=Phase displays the phase-response of the field or spectrum in degree.

BodeType=Real, BodeType=Imag

These settings displays the real- or imaginary part of the field or spectrum. The unit is according to the observation point. In ABEC the output is a peak-value.

Note on Spectral Curves

Because spectral curves are rendered with the help of VACS settings of BodeType= are default values of display. You can change the Bode-Type in VACS with the help of its Range Dialogs.
