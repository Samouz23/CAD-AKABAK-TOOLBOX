Identifier hD1=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Height or depth of a dome or dust-cap.
Used in Diaphragm, Shell
See also Diaphragm shapes, tD1=

hD1= is the height of the dome in the center of the Diaphragm or the Shell.

A positive value creates a convex shape and a negative value a concave shape.
