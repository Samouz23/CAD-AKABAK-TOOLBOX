DrvWeight=

Home » Scripts

Identifier DrvWeight=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Amplitude weighing of driving
Used in Diaphragm, Pressure_Points, Pressure_Box, VibFile, Driving
See also DrvDelay=, Driving Groups

DrvWeight= allows to modify the amplitude of driving during solving stage (Boundary Element Method) or in Direct Sound Scripts (Direct Sound Mode).

In sections with a list of individual driving assignments, such as Driving and Pressure_Points the parameter DrvWeight= forms the default value for all driving elements or pressure points.
