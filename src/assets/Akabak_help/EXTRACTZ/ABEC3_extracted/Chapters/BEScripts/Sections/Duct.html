Duct works as an element-factory, which conveniently creates boundary elements for waveguides of constant cross-section of shape given by Ducttype=.

The Duct is placed with the help of parameter Position=. Node P1 is the center of the first aperture of the Duct. P1, P2 and P3 should form a triangle which normal points on-axis. The axial rotation is given by the vector P2 - P1.

Parameter Len= is the length of the waveguide.

The Duct can be equipped with flanges with the help of parameter NumBaffles=.

By default Normals are pointing into the duct-interior. Use SwapNormals in order to invert the Normals.


Example

  Duct  "D1"
    DuctType=Ellipse
    WD=160mm;  HD=100mm
    Len=320mm
    Position=x
    NumBaffles=2

This section creates boundary elements for a waveguide of constant elliptically shaped cross-section. Position=x means it is located with its one aperture at the origin and is pointing along the x-axis. Because of NumBaffles=2 there will be a flange at both apertures.
