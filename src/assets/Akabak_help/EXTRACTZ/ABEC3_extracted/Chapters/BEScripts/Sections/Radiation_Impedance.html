Section Radiation_Impedance calculates the lumped self- and mutual- radiation impedance spectra of driving-groups as specified in the Boundary Element Solving Script. See chapter Radiation Impedance for background information.

Section Radiation_Impedance consists of a general part and a List of Radiation Impedance Items. Each item yields one impedance-spectrum of Driving Groups.

Parameter RadImpType= controls normalization and scaling of the impedance. Its default value is RadImpType=Normalized.

Parameter BodeType= controls the initial view of the curve in VACS, which, however, can be altered sub-sequentially.
