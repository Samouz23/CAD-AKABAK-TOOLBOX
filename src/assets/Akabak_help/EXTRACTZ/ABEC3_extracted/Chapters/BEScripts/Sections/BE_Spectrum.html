Section BE_Spectrum yields the calculation of sound-pressure response at various spatial points.

For the Boundary Element Analysis and if Control_Spectrum has been specified then BE_Spectrum can render more abscissa points then was actually solved for at the Boundary Element Analysis. See also chapter on Interpolation.

For Direct Sound Analysis section Control_Spectrum should define a valid frequency abscissa.

PlotType= controls mainly the graphical rendering, for example whether you want a curve- or a contour-plot. It also controls the distribution of field-points.
