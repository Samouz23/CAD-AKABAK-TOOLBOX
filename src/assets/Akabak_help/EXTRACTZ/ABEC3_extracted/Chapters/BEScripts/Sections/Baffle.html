The Baffle component eventually creates Boundary Elements for you. In this sense it is similar to the Diaphragm-component.

The purpose of the Baffle is to facilitate specification of geometry.

The Baffle is a flat plane of triangular or quadrilateral shape. The outer edges are defined either with the help of a Position= and dimension (dB=, WB=, HB=), or with the help of Vertices=.

On the Baffle other components can be arranged using local coordinates. These local coordinates are in 2D and measured from the mounting point, which is the first point as specified at parameters Vertices= or Position=, respectively.

The Baffle is thought to be an acoustic boundary and, hence, has no thickness or any other structural features. This means that, for a real baffle you would typically implement two Baffle-components, one for the front and one for the rear.
