The Elements-sections are the most important part for the boundary element analysis, as these sections specify the planes of the boundaries. You can specify as many Elements-sections as you like. Typically multiple Elements-sections help to structure the script. The display of each Elements-section can be manipulated with the help of the tree-list of the desktop.

If the Nodes and Elements approach is used then an Elements-section takes reference to a Nodes-section.

Alternatively or in a mixed fashion, triangles of a mesh-files can be linked. Because of this you need to specify RefNodes= and/or MeshFileAlias=.

Elements-section can be referenced by other sections such as WallImpedance or Driving. In this case each Elements-section needs a unique name. Naming is also advantageous for desktop-control and error-messages.

Elements-sections have two parts. In the first part parameters are specified, such as RefNodes="N". The second part is the list of Boundary Element-definitions.
