Ratio=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Ratio=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Coupling-factor
Used in Coupler

The Coupler sets in relation the potentials and flows of the primary side to that of the secondary side:

Uvu/Uts = Ratio

Is/Iu = -1/Ratio

The potential and the flow, can be substituted by the voltage U and the current I, the force F and the velocity v or by the pressure p and the volume velocity V, respectively. The primary and the secondary side need not to be of the same domain.

Specifications

Coupler allows a variety of ways to specify Ratio.


1) Directly: There is not unit. For example

  Coupler
    Node=1=2=3
    Ratio=127.33

2) Formula: You can specify a complex runtime formula instead of a number. There are two variables available:

  f
  w

with f abscissa frequency in [f] = Hz and w = ω = 2πf

The formula-system should be encapsulated in { }. The result of the last formula is assigned to Ratio=. The result can be real- or complex-valued. For example

  Coupler
    Node=1=2=3
    Ratio={ fp = 5e-3;  dD=100e-3;
            SD = pi*sqr(dD/2);
            SD_f = SD/(1 + (f/fp)^2);
            Result = 1/SD_f }

3) Diaphragm: For convenience, it is possible to specify Ratio via a value for the diaphragm-area. Internally following calculation is performed: Ratio = 1/SD.

  Coupler
    Node=1=2=3
    SD=7.854e-3m2
