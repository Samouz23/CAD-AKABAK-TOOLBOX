Identifier eta=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Damping factor of duct or enclosure
Used in Duct, Enclosure

eta= is a damping factor of a wave through a duct or within an enclosure.

p2/p1 = exp(-η·d)

with distance d between two points of pressure p1 and p2. η is part of the complex wavenumber:

k = k' - jη

Large values of η yield strong damping. Typical values are

η Damping
1.0e-5 low
1.0e-3 medium
0.1 high

etaD= and etaB=

In the vented enclosure there are two damping constants available. One for the enclosure (etaB=) and one for the vent (etaD=). For enclosures eta = etaB.

Relation to Acoustic Resistance

In chapter Ra= we have expressions for the acoustic resistance. This can be related to η

η = Ra·SD/(L·ρ·c)

with air-density ρ, speed of sound c. This formula derives from the transfer-function of a duct with cross-sectional area of SD and length L. Parameter L cancels with similar parameter in the formula of Ra=. SD is then the effective cross-section area of the damping material.

Derivation: We start with the driving point function of the open duct:

Za = jρc/SD·tan(k·L)

with wave-number k = ω/c. Expanding into Taylor series gives

Za = jρc/SD·(k·L + (k·L)3/3 + ...)

Taking only the linear term and by substitution of the lossy wavenumber k = k' - jη we can identify the series connection of lumped acoustic resistance and mass, where the acoustic resistance is

Ra = L/SD·ρ·c·η

Akabaks' Q/f0 parameter

QD_f0= and QB_f0= are regarded depreciated. They still work but will not be documented.

In Akabak we specified QD_f0= or QB_f0=, which is inverse proportional to η=. The Qs are more related to second order circuits and coil quality factors, whereas η is home to waveguides.

The relation to η is

Q/f0 = 2π/c·1/η  ≈  1/(55·η)
