Identifier Bandwidth=
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Bandwidth of band-pass transformation.
Used in Filter

Bandwidth= belongs to the parameter-set of standard filter alignments.

This parameter controls the relative bandwidth of the band-pass transformation. For example

    Filter 'F14'
      f0=1kHz
      Order=6   BU  Bandpass   Bandwidth=2

Bandwidth is defined as

Bandwidth = (f2 - f1)/f0

with f1 and f2 lower and upper cut-off frequency and f0 filter-frequency.
