Identifier WTh= HTh=
Units m
Format Floating point number
Purpose Width and Height of throat cross-section.
Used in Waveguide
See also dTh=, STh=, dTh1=, STh1=
Lumped Element Diaphragms

WTh= and HTh= are the width and height of a rectangular or elliptical cross-section at the throat of the waveguide.
