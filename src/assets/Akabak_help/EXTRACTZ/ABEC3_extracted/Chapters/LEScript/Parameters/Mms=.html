Mms=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Mms=
Units kg
Format Floating point number
Purpose Mass of moving assembly.
Used in Def_Driver, Def_TwoCoilsDriver, Def_PiezoDriver, Def_Speaker, Diaphragm
See also Mechanics of Driver

Mms is the total in-vacuum mass of the vibrating assembly of the transducer. The lumped element mass, Mms is used in the Standard Model of the Electro-Dyn-Model.

For example

  Def_Driver 'SP38'
    Re=5.82ohm   Le=2.9mH   fre=1.1kHz  ExpoRe=0.824  ExpoLe=0.756
    BL=21Tm
    Mms=70g  Cms=81e-6m/N  Rms=1Ns/m
