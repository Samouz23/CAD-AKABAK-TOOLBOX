Identifier Visc=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Viscosity-factor.
Used in Duct, Enclosure (vented)

Visc= can be used to magnify or diminish the damping factor due to viscosity.

Visc > 1     Amplification

Visc = 1     Original

Visc < 1     Diminishing

Visc = -1     Switched off

Viscosity yields damping and is performed in Duct and Enclosure components by default. As can be seen in the formula given at parameter Ra= the damping due to viscosity and thermal exchange depends on the perimeter of the duct. Visc=, finally, is simply a multiplier, which increases Ra.

Specifying Visc=-1 will cause skipping the viscosity calculation. In this case you need to specify eta=.
