Identifier Ripple=
Units dB
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Ripple of CH-alignment
Used in Filter

Ripple= belongs to the parameter-set of standard filter alignments.

Ripple= controls the level of pass-band ripple of a Chebychev (CH) alignment. See also chapter Alignment Types. The above example displays amplitudes of Ripple=3dB, Ripple=6dB and Ripple=9dB. The order of the CH-lowpass is Order=6.
