Identifier { b0 ; ... a0= ; a1= ; ... }
Format { Formula }
Purpose Coefficients of Laplace Transfer Function
Used in Filter
See also Examples

bi and ai are real valued coefficients of the transfer-function of the Filter-element.

The coefficients are embedded in a Formula-system, which allows for arithmetic.

bi= are the coefficients or the numerator and ai are the coefficients of the denominator of the transfer-function.

bi Numerator any real valued number
ai Denominator real valued number > 0

i is the index of the power of the frequency variable. The range of i = 0 ... n with n the maximum power of the polynomial. In ABEC n <= 30 because of numerical limitations of the computer.

Specification

Coefficients are entered with the help of a formula-system. The formula-system is written in curly brackets and can go over several lines. There is no identifier.

The coefficients of the numerator are called b0=, b1=, b2=,... and those of the denominator a0=, a1=, a2=,.... These function names are reserved here. Their values are entered in the usual way, e.g.: b0=1. However, each entry has to be separated by a semicolon. A space is permitted before and after the equal sign.

Any coefficients not entered are set to zero by default. They may be entered in any order. In addition to entering numerical values, you may also assign expressions, symbolic constants or previously defined coefficients.

Comments inside the curly brackets are also permitted. Comments commence with // and end at the end of the line. Constants that have previously been computed in the definition Def_Const can also be assigned. The values of several filters can thus be changed from a central point.

The values calculated for the coefficients have to be real values. However, the formula system allows for complex calculation, which means you can calculate coefficients from complex zeros, for example.
