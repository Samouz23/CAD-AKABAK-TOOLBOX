Identifier Ls=
Units H
Format Floating point number
Purpose Series inductance.
Used in Capacitor

Ls= is the series inductance of the Capacitor component. Units of [Ls] = H (Henry).

By default Ls = 0.

For example

  Capacitor  'C1'
    Node=10=20
    C=1uF  Ls=1uH  Rs=0.5ohm
