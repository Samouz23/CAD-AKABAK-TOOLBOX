Rms=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Rms=
Units Ns/m
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Mechanic resistance of suspensions
Used in Def_Driver, Def_TwoCoilsDriver, Def_Speaker, Def_PiezoDriver, Diaphragm
See also Mechanics of Driver

The parameter Rms of the Standard Model of the Electro-Dyn-Model specifies the damping at the fundamental resonance fs. This model assumes Rms has been measured in-vacuum and is mainly caused within the suspensions.

For example

  Def_Driver 'SP38'
    Re=5.82ohm   Le=2.9mH   fre=1.1kHz  ExpoRe=0.824  ExpoLe=0.756
    BL=21Tm
    Mms=70g  Cms=81e-6m/N  Rms=1Ns/m
