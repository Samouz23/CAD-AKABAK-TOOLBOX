Cms=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Cms=
Units m/N
Format Floating-point number
Purpose Mechanic compliance of suspensions
Used in Def_Driver, Def_TwoCoilsDriver, Def_Speaker, Def_PiezoDriver, Diaphragm
See also Mechanics of Driver, Kms=

Cms is the total lumped compliance of all suspensions as used in the Standard Model of the Electro-Dyn-Model. The total compliance is usually the parallel of the compliances of the outer suspension of the diaphragm, Cms2 and of the spider, Cms1.

For example

  Def_Driver 'SP38'
    Re=5.82ohm   Le=2.9mH   fre=1.1kHz  ExpoRe=0.824  ExpoLe=0.756
    BL=21Tm
    Mms=70g  Cms=81e-6m/N  Rms=1Ns/m

Kms

The compliance Cms is the inverse of the stiffness, Kms=, which can be specified alternatively.

Cms = 1/Kms

For example

  Def_Driver 'SP38'
    Re=5.82ohm   Le=2.9mH   fre=1.1kHz  ExpoRe=0.824  ExpoLe=0.756
    BL=21Tm
    Mms=70g  Kms=12345.67N/m  Rms=1Ns/m
