Purpose Electrical quality factor

See also Electro-dynamic-model

The electrical quality factor, Qes is part of the total quality factor Qts of the electro-dynamic standard model of a loudspeaker transducer.

with

Mms mechanical vacuum mass of vibrating assembly.

Cms mechanical compliance of all suspensions.

Re electric resistance of voice coil.

BL force-factor of electro-dynamic motor.


The smaller Qes the stronger the damping of the fundamental resonance at fs.

Qes= is used for transducers with a single voice-coil, such as Def_Driver and Def_Speaker.

Qes1= and Qes2= are used for transducers with two voice-coils, such as Def_TwoCoilsDriver.
