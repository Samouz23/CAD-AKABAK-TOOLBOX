Identifier mb=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Mass-factor of speaker.
Used in Def_Speaker

mb= is a correction factor for the vacuum resonance frequency fs= if the speaker is mounted in a sealed enclosure due to acoustic mass-load.

fsb = mb·fs

The simple lumped element model of Def_Speaker uses fsb as the resonance of the speaker.

Typical range is mb = 0.9 ... 1.0

Note, mb= is a very approximate means to take into account the complex radiation impedance the speaker encounters if placed in an enclosure. For a heavy diaphragm you may ignore mb=. If you do not use the enclosure model of Def_Speaker mb= should also be ignored.
