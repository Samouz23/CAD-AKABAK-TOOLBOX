Re= Re1= Re2=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Re= Re1= Re2=
Units ohm
Format Floating point number
Purpose Voice coil resistance.
Used in Def_Driver, Def_TwoCoilsDriver, Def_Speaker
See also Electro-dynamic-model

Re= is the value of the voice-coil resistance as explained in chapter Voice Coil Model.

Re1= and Re2= are used for an electro-dynamic driver with two voice coils, see Def_TwoCoilsDriver.


Example of a driver with one voice-coil

  Def_Driver 'SP38'
    Re=5.82ohm   Le=2.9mH   fre=1.1kHz  ExpoRe=0.824  ExpoLe=0.756
    BL=21N/A
    Mms=70g  Cms=81e-6m/N  Rms=1Ns/m

Example of a driver with two voice-coils

  Def_TwoCoilsDriver 'SP382'
    Re1=5.82ohm   Le1=2.9mH   fre1=1.1kHz  ExpoRe1=0.824  ExpoLe1=0.756
    BL1=21N/A
    Re2=5.82ohm   Le2=2.9mH   fre2=1.1kHz  ExpoRe2=0.824  ExpoLe2=0.756
    BL2=21N/A
    Mms=70g  Cms=81e-6m/N  Rms=1Ns/m
