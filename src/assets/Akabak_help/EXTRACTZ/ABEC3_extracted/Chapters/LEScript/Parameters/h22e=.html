h22e=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier h22e=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Differential-output-admittance
Used in Transistor
See also h11e=, h12e=, h21e=

h22e= is the output admittance of the Transistor, FET and Valve at a certain point of operation. Units [h11e] = Siemens. Typically ignored.

h22e = ∂IC/∂UCE with UBE = const

h22e= is approximately related to the Early-voltage:

h22e ≈ ∂IC/Uy

with Uy the so-called Early-voltage. Uy = 80 ... 200V for npn-transistors, Uy = 40 ... 150V for pnp transistors. ∂IC is the collector-current at the operating point.


Example: Transistor BC107A in collector-circuit (collector to ground, nodes t=0).

Operating point: UCE = 5V, IC = 2mA, ft = 1kHz

  Transistor  'Q1'
    Node=1=0=2
    h11e=8.7kohm  h12e=1.5e-4
    h21e=220      h22e=18uS
    ft=200MHz     Ccb=3.5pF
