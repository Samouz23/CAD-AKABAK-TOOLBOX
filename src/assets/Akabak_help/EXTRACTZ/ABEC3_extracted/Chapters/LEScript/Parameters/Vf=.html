Identifier Vf=
Units m3
Format Floating point number
Purpose Effective volume of a compression chamber
Used in Duct, Waveguide

Vf= is the effective volume of an optional compression chamber at one of the vents of the Duct or at the throat of the Waveguide component. It is there for convenience. Alternatively, use an Enclosure component of volume Vb = Vf.
