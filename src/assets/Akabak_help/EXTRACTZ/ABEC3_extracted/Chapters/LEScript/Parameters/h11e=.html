h11e=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier h11e=
Units ohm
Format Floating point number
Purpose Differential-base-emitter resistance rBE
Used in Transistor
See also h12e=, h21e=, h22e=

h11e= is the input resistance of the Transistor, FET and Valve certain point of operation. Units [h11e] = ohm. Typically ignored for FET and Valve.

h11e = ∂RBE = ∂UBE/∂IB with UCE = const

∂UBE is the base-emitter voltage, ∂IB the current into the base and ∂RBE is the base-emitter resistance. All values at a certain point of operation.


Example: Transistor BC107A in collector-circuit (collector to ground, nodes t=0).

Operating point: UCE = 5V, IC = 2mA, ft = 1kHz

    Node=1=0=2

    h21e=220      h22e=18uS
    ft=200MHz     Ccb=3.5pF
