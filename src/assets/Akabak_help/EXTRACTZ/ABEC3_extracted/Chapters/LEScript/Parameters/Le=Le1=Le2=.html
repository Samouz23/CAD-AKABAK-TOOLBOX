Le= Le1= Le2=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier Le= Le1= Le2=
Units H
Format Floating point number
Purpose Voice coil inductance.
Used in Def_Driver, Def_TwoCoilsDriver, Def_Speaker
See also Le2=, Le21=, Le22=, ExpoLe=, ExpoLe1=, ExpoLe2=

Le is the inductance of the voice-coil of the electro-dynamic transducer as used in the standard model of a loudspeaker.

At higher frequencies the voice coil model should be extended.

Le= is used for transducers with a single voice-coil, such as Def_Driver and Def_Speaker.

Le1= and Le2= are used for transducers with two voice-coils, such as Def_TwoCoilsDriver.


Example of a driver with one voice-coil

  Def_Driver  'P1'
    Re=6.74ohm   Le=1.102mH
    Le2=0.780mH  Re2=1.57ohm  // Klippel VC-model
    fs=25.2Hz   Qms=1.567
    Mms=28g                   // special Rohacel diaphragm
    BL=11.2Tm
