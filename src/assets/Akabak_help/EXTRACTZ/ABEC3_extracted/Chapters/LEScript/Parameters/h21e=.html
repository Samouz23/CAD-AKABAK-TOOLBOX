h21e=

Identifier h21e=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Differential current gain β
Used in Transistor
See also h11e=, h12e=, h22e=

h21e= is the current gain β at a certain point of operation. h21e has no unit and should always be specified.

h21e = β = ∂IC/∂IB with UCE = const

In the case of the FET or the valve, h21e is equal to the forward trans-conductance g normalized to one Siemens.


Example: Transistor BC107A in collector-circuit (collector to ground, nodes t=0).

Operating point: UCE = 5V, IC = 2mA, ft = 1kHz

  Transistor  'Q1'
    Node=1=0=2
    h11e=8.7kohm  h12e=1.5e-4
    h21e=220      h22e=18uS
    ft=200MHz     Ccb=3.5pF
