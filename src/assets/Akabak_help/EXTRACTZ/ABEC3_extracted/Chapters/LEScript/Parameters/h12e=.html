h12e=

Home » LE-Scripts

Identifier h12e=
Format Floating point number
Purpose Voltage-reaction-factor Ar
Used in Transistor
See also h11e=, h21e=, h22e=

h12e= is the voltage-reaction-factor Ar. The absolute value of h12e is often less than 10-4 and can usually be neglected (h12e=0).

h12e = ∂UBE/∂UCE with IB = const

At higher frequencies, however, the reaction increases, which is taken into account via the collector-base capacitance Ccb=.


Example: Transistor BC107A in collector-circuit (collector to ground, nodes t=0).

Operating point: UCE = 5V, IC = 2mA, ft = 1kHz

  Transistor  'Q1'
    Node=1=0=2
    h11e=8.7kohm  h12e=1.5e-4
    h21e=220      h22e=18uS
    ft=200MHz     Ccb=3.5pF
