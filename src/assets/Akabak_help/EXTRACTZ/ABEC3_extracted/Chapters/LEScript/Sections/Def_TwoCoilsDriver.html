Def_TwoCoilsDriver defines an electro-dynamic driver with two voice-coils. It is referenced by the six-pole network-element Driver.

Def_TwoCoilsDriver is operated very similar to Def_Driver only that there are two motors available.

Def_TwoCoilsDriver can be driven via both voice coils. For example, both port can be wired in parallel or in series. Or, there can be connected electronic components for a more sophisticated drive. It can also be driven just by one voice coil, whereas the other is used to observe the voltage feedbacked.
