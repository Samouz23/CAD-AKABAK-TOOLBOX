The Ob-Field-page provides control for the display of observation fields as specified by script-section Field.

Field List

The tree-list at the top of the page shows all available field specifications of all registered observation scripts.

Checked items are visible. All fields are calculated - visible and invisible. Alternatively consider Observation/Fields on page Views.

Frequency List

The tree-list at the bottom of the page shows all available frequencies for which fields can be calculated. Only checked frequencies will be calculated.

In Boundary Element Analysis the frequency grid stems from the frequency definition at section Control_Solver of the solving script. In Direct Sound Analysis frequencies are generated by the specification at section Control_Field of the observation script.


After calculation of field-values you need select a frequency for displaying (menu Fields/Show Field).

Single Frequencies

To display the response of a particular frequency select its entry in the frequency-list and issue menu Fields/Show Field. Alternatively, dbl-click the entry or press ENTER.

Range of Frequencies

ABEC can display the amplitude-mean-value of a range of frequencies. For this first select a range of list-items in the frequency-list. Then issue menu Fields/Show Field. In order to select a range of list items first select the start-item. Then press SHIFT and select the end-item.
