After issuing menu Fields/Start Calculation or Spectra/Start Calculation ABEC outputs information about meshing, frequencies, acoustic parameters and so on.

If an error occurs a red flagged icon is placed on the tab of the page.

The calculation can be stopped at any time via menu Fields/Abort Calculation or Spectra/Abort Calculation, respectively. The termination may take a while until all sub-threads are cleared.
