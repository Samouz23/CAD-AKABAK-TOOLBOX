For comfortably editing scripts, we recommend to use a powerful text-editor, best with multiple-file support. For example UltraEdit or Notepad++.

In ABEC open menu Options/Preferences/Editor where you can provide the link to the editor of your preference. Then this editor-application will be called whenever you issue menu Edit/Edit Script.

After installation you find a collection of syntax-files at

  C:\Users\(User)\Documents\RDTeam\ABEC3\Syntax Highligthing\
