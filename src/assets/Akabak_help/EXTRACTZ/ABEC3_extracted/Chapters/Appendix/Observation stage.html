At Observation Stage we calculate the frequency response and display it as a Field or as a Spectrum.

If we engage the Boundary Element Method then observation can take place only after solving-stage. The Direct Sound and the pure Lumped Element Analysis do not have a solving stage, and, hence, we can observe directly. See also table Operation-Modes.

Acoustic fields are specified with the help of section Field of the observation script. The output is done in the 3D-Viever of ABEC.

Spectral observations are specified in a dedicated observation script. For BEM- or Direct Sound acoustics the section is BE_Spectrum. For radiation impedance the section is called Radiation_Impedance. For observation of Lumped Element parameters the section is LE_Spectrum. Spectral output is automatically sent to VACS, which another application assumed to be installed on the computer. It is also possible to render spectral output to the clipboard or to a file, see menu Spectra/Output Ways.
