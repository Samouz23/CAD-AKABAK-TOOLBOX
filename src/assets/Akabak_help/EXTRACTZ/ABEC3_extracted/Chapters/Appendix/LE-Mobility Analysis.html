The Lumped Element Solver of ABEC assumes simply a network of potentials and flows. For the solver it does not matter, which physical parameters are actually meant by the potential and the flow. It can be a voltage, force or pressure and current, velocity or volume-velocity. However, other configurations are possible, too, and can yield meaningful results.

The mechanical Lumped Element Analysis is easiest if we swap network-parameters for the mechanical domain. By default the potential=force and flow=velocity. However, the Mobility Analysis symbolizes the potential=velocity and flow=force. ABEC supports this analysis with a range of mechanical components and special observation points.
