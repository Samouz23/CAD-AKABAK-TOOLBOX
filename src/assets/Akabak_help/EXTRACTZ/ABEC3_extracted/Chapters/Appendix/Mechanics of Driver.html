Electro Dynamic Components

The mechanics of a transducer for electroacoustic is simple, there are no gear-boxes, no levelers and no rotational parts. Typically, mechanical motion is controlled translation of the diaphragm. Its motor is usually rigidly fixed to the membrane.

In all models of ABEC we assume rigid body motion of the diaphragm. Also we ignore rotational motion (toggling and twisting).

Surface vibration can be taken into account with the help of vibration- files, see section VibFile. More information, see chapter Lumped Element Diaphragms.

Fundamental Parameters

Mms

Mms is the total lumped mass of the vibrating assembly. The unit of Mms is

[Mms] = kg

Normally, we assume Mms to be the mass, which is measured in vacuum. This is so because ABEC will provide the radiation impedance during simulation.

Cms

Cms is the effective compliance of all suspensions and possible other springiness. The unit of Cms is

[Cms] = m/N

Alternatively, any component, which has Cms as a parameter, also accepts stiffness as input:

Kms = 1/Cms

The unit of Kms is

[Kms] = N/m

Rms

Rms is a mechanical resistance assumed to be active inside the springs. The unit of Rms is

[Rms] = Ns/m
