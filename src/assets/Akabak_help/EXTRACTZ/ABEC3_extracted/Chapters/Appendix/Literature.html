There are a vast number of excellent books and papers on the topic of the Boundary Element Method. However, many of these articles would discuss some interesting refinement or complicated mathematics. It is often difficult to find the beginning of the string. The author, for example, started with the following two books, and sub-sequentially worked through a pile of papers, each giving hints and clues on the topic.
