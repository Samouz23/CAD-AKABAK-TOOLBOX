Units

Home

In BEM-scripts units are optional and typically are applicable at parameters, which expect a floating point value. If no unit is specified then SI-units are assumed. Unit-specification is case-sensitive.

Distance Units

In this case enter the full unit such as "mm" for milli-meter, "cm" for centi-meter and "m" for meter. It is also possible to specify inch and foot. For example:

    0.0254m      2.54cm
    25.4mm       25400um
    1in          0.0833ft

Area Units

Also here enter the full unit such as "mm2" for square milli-meter, "cm2" for square centi-meter and "m2" for square meter. It is also possible to specify square inch and square foot. For example:

    0.00064516m2      6.4516cm2
    645.16mm2         645160000um2
    1in2              0.00693889ft2

Time Units

Time values can be entered in SCI- or ENG-format or with the unit. Hence Delay=1m is identical to Delay=1ms.

Frequency Units

Frequency values can be entered in SCI- or ENG-format or with the unit.

For example: Frequencies=10Hz, 200Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz

Or Alternatively: Frequencies=10, 200, 1k, 2k
