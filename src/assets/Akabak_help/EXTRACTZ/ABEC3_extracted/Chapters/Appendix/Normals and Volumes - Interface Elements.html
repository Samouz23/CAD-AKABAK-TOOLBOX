Normals and Volumes - Interface Elements

Home » Normals and Volumes

Interfaces are part of Sub-Domain Modeling. In an Elements-section, which connects two sub-domains, say 1 and 2, all planes become so-called interface-elements:


  Elements "I1"
    Subdomain= 1, 2
    RefNodes=N
    101   1001  1002  1003
    102   1005  1006  1007  1008
    103   MeshFileAlias=M1  Include=7..10
    ...

For each plane, ie boundary element, which is listed in the Elements-section, ABEC makes sure that sound pressure and normal velocity fit on both sides of each interface-element at its center-point.


Also with interfaces, the orientation of the normals is of importance. An Elements-section with interfaces belongs to two sub-domains as becomes obvious by the specification Subdomain= 1, 2.


The interface-normal should point into the sub-domain
indicated by the first index of Subdomain=, in our example "1".



Example


  Subdomain_Properties 'Enclosure'
    Subdomain=1
    ElType=Interior

  Subdomain_Properties 'Vent'
    Subdomain=2
    ElType=Interior

  Subdomain_Properties 'Exterior'
    Subdomain=3
    ElType=Exterior  // Radiation to infinity
  ...
  Elements "I-Enclosure-Vent"
    RefNodes="N"
    Subdomain=1,2       // Connecting sub-domain 1 and 2
    101   2016  2017  2018  2015
  ...
  Elements "I-Vent-Exterior"
    RefNodes="N"
    Subdomain=2,3       // Connecting sub-domain 2 and 3
    101   2012  2013  2014  2011
  ...

In the above example there are three sub-domains: Subdomain=1 is the interior of the cabinet of a vented loudspeaker-enclosure. Subdomain=2 is the sub-domain of the inner of the vent. Subdomain=3 is the sub-domain of the exterior radiation domain.

The normals of interface-elements of section "I-Enclosure-Vent" should point into the cabinet volume because the first index of Subdomain = 1, 2 relates to Subdomain=1, which is the interior of the cabinet.

The normals of interface-elements of section "I-Vent-Exterior" should point into the volume of the vent because the first index of Subdomain = 2, 3 relates to Subdomain=2, which is the vent interior.

Alternative

Alternatively, the following specification would yield identical results.

  ...
  Elements "I-Enclosure-Vent"
    RefNodes="N"
    Subdomain=2,1  | Connecting sub-domain 2 and 1
    SwapNormals
    101   2016  2017  2018  2015
  ...
  Elements "I-Vent-Exterior"
    RefNodes="N"
    Subdomain=3,2  | Connecting sub-domain 3 and 2
    SwapNormals
    101   2012  2013  2014  2011
  ...

Now, in "I-Enclosure-Vent" the first index relates to Subdomain=2, which is the interior of the vent. Because the normals of the interface should always point into the domain, we can either rearrange the order of node-indices or use the command SwapNormals in order to conveniently invert the direction of the normals.

Similar, in section "I-Vent-Exterior". The first index relates to Subdomain=3, which is the external volume, and because the interface-normal should point into the domain - here the whole external space - we have to rearrange the direction of the normals.
