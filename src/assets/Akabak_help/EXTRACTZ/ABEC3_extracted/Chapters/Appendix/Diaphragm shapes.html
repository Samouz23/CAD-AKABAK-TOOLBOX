The Diaphragm-section allows for a variety of shapes. The most simple shape is a circular disc and the most complex is the diaphragm of a conical loudspeaker driver.

Depending on how many parameters are specified the shape will morph from one to the other.

Some parameters can be negative (hD1 or dVC) and as such will yield the concave version or a hole, respectively. See also Diaphragm Case-Tree.


In this chapter: Outer Shapes, Loudspeaker Cone, Loudspeaker Dome

Outer Shapes

dD= is the outer diameter of a circular diaphragm (without the suspension).

WD= and HD= are the width and height of a non-circular shape (without the suspension).

RectType= Rectangle, Ellipse or Oval controls the detail of the shape.

SD= or SD1= specify the projected area and will be converted to dD, WD=HD or dD1, respectively.

dD1= specifies an inner diameter.

dD1 > zero is the diameter of an inner dome (see below).

dD1 < zero is the inner diameter of an annulus.

ws= is the width of an outer suspension. On the suspension the velocity is distributed linearly. At the inner edge the velocity is at 100% and at the outer edge at zero.

Loudspeaker Cone

Diaphragm creates a cone if the cone-depth tD1= is unequal zero. Positive values create a concave cone otherwise it is convex. tD1 measures at the front side of the diaphragm and from the level of the suspension down to the base of the dust-cap.

The diaphragm is rendered differently for the front and rear side, as explained at parameter Side=.

Cone Front

hD1= is the height of the dust-cap. If hD1 > zero then the dome is rendered convex, if hD1=0 flat and if hD1 < 0 its shape is concave (see below).

Removing the dome can be achieved with dVC < zero (see at Cone Rear).

Cone Rear

hD2= is the height of the total cone, which typically goes beyond the dust-cap, therefore hD2 >= tD1.

dVC= is the diameter of the voice-coil assumed here to be identical to the base of the cone.

If dVC < zero then the front-side of the Diaphragm will have a hole.

hVC= is the height of the voice-coil former, which contributes to the boundary of the acoustic field.

dM= is the diameter of the magnet.

hM= is the height of the magnet.

Cone with Concave Dome

If hD1 < zero then a concave dust-cap is rendered.

Loudspeaker Dome

The Diaphragm becomes a dome if only the outer diameter dD= and the height hD1= is specified. For positive values of hD1 the dome is convex, for negative values it is concave.
